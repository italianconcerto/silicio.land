silicio.land collects notes on AI, software, cybersecurity, and technology. Economics occasionally makes an appearance too: behind a technical choice there is almost always a cost, an incentive, and someone who would rather pass them on to somebody else.

I write when I have a thesis, an experiment, or a mistake worth documenting. No editorial calendar. If an idea fits into three bullet points and a predictable conclusion, it probably does not deserve a post.

The site is HTML and CSS. Few dependencies, no banners, no pop-ups.
